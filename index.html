✦ Explore the Cosmos

Astronomy

Gaze beyond the horizon. The universe is vast, ancient, and endlessly fascinating — and we're only just beginning to understand it.

🌌

The Milky Way

Our home galaxy contains an estimated 100–400 billion stars and stretches roughly 100,000 light-years across. At its center lies a supermassive black hole called Sagittarius A*.

Galaxy
🪐

Saturn's Rings

Saturn's iconic rings are made of ice and rock, ranging from tiny grains to chunks as large as a house. They span up to 282,000 km but are only about 10 meters thick in places.

Solar System
⭐

Neutron Stars

When a massive star collapses, it can form a neutron star — an object just 20 km across but with more mass than our Sun. A teaspoon of its material would weigh a billion tons.

Stellar
🔭

James Webb Telescope

The JWST peers deeper into space than ever before, capturing light from galaxies formed just a few hundred million years after the Big Bang, reshaping our view of cosmic history.

Exploration
☄️

Dark Matter

About 27% of the universe is dark matter — invisible, undetected directly, yet its gravitational influence shapes the structure of every galaxy and cluster we observe.

Cosmology
🌠

Exoplanets

As of 2026, astronomers have confirmed over 5,700 exoplanets. Some orbit in the "habitable zone" of their stars — the region where liquid water could exist on the surface.
